01What Is Social Strand?

It's where you share your work, follow other members, react and comment on posts, and message each other directly or in groups — all in one place, alongside your KFS account tools (My Movies, My Works, Collaborate).

This guide covers every feature currently available, how your data is protected, and the rules that keep Social Strand a place people want to be in — including what happens if those rules aren't followed.

02Feed & Posts

The main Social Strand feed shows posts from members you follow and the wider community, newest first. New posts from other members appear as a small "↑ New posts" pill at the top of the feed rather than being inserted automatically — tap it whenever you're ready so you're never pulled away from something you're reading.

2.1 Post types

Project post — share a finished or in‑progress project with a caption, cover image, and optional collaborators.
Text post — a caption‑only update with no attached media.
Video post — a project post with an embedded video and its own caption field.
Collab post — an open call for collaborators, posted from the Collaborate tab, with role, description and fulfillment date.

2.2 Creating and editing a post

Use the composer to pick a post type, add a title, tags, description, and (for project/video posts) media. Text boxes grow automatically as you type, so long captions are never trapped in a tiny scrolling box.

You can edit or delete your own posts at any time from the post's menu (⋯). Deleting a post removes it from the feed, your profile, and anyone's saved links to it.

2.3 Reactions & comments

Tap and hold (or long‑press) the reaction icon on a post to choose from the reaction set (❤ 😂 😮 😢 😡 👏 🔥 😍 👍 🙂 😊), or tap once for a quick default reaction. Comments open in the post's detail view, support threaded replies, and refresh automatically every few seconds while the detail view is open — so you'll see new comments from others without needing to close and reopen the post.

Note — Comment auto‑refresh briefly pauses while your cursor is inside the reply box, so nothing you're typing gets overwritten.

2.4 Following & analytics

Follow members from their posts to see more of their work in your feed. Your own profile shows a Followers / Following list and basic post analytics (views, reactions, comments) so you can see how your posts are performing.

2.5 My Movies / My Works

Movie submissions and your works portfolio live together under one panel with a tab switcher ("Submissions" / "My Works"), since both track the same thing — your film credits with KFS. From here you can submit new work and request edits to existing entries.

03Your Data & Message Security

This is the part most members want a straight answer on, so here it is in plain language before we get into features.

3.1 How your DMs and group chats are protected

Every direct message and group chat on Social Strand is end‑to‑end encrypted (E2EE). In practice, that means:

Your device generates its own private encryption key when you first sign in. That private key never leaves your device — it is never uploaded, backed up, or transmitted to KFS in any form.
Only the matching public key is shared with the server, so other members' devices can encrypt messages to you. A public key can encrypt messages for you but cannot be used to read them — decryption requires the private key sitting only on your device.
Messages are encrypted on the sender's device before they ever leave it. The server stores and relays ciphertext — scrambled data it cannot itself read.
Messages are decrypted only on the recipient's device, at the moment they're displayed. This applies equally to one‑to‑one DMs and group chats.

3.2 What this means for you

  • Nobody at KFS can casually open your inbox and browse your conversations — there is nothing readable to browse on the server side.
  • A conversation only exists in plaintext on the screens of the people actually in it.
  • Turning off Read Receipts, setting a nickname, or customizing your chat appearance are all local, per‑device preferences layered on top of this — they don't affect encryption either way.

3.3 The one deliberate exception: reporting

Encryption is designed to protect you — not to protect someone who's harassing or abusing you. So there is exactly one narrow, user‑initiated exception, modelled on the same approach used by mainstream E2EE apps like Signal:

When you choose to report a specific DM or group message, that single message is decrypted on your own device, at the moment you press submit.
The decrypted copy is attached to your report so a moderator can review the exact content you flagged — not your whole conversation history, and not anything you haven't reported.
Only KFS's master administrators — the small, accountable team responsible for trust & safety — can view decrypted report content, and only for the message(s) included in that report.
Note — KFS cannot proactively scan, read, or monitor the plaintext content of your DMs or group chats. The only way a message becomes visible to a moderator is if a participant in that conversation reports it.

3.4 Data KFS does and doesn't hold

KFS can see / storesKFS cannot see
That a conversation exists, and its participantsThe plaintext content of your DMs or group messages
Encrypted (ciphertext) message data, needed to deliver it to the right deviceYour private encryption key, at any point
Ordinary account data — profile, posts, comments, reactions (these are not E2EE, since they're public by design)Messages you haven't reported, even during a moderation investigation
The specific content of a message once you report itAnything beyond the exact message(s) included in a report
Note — Posts, comments, and reactions on the Social Strand feed are intentionally public‑facing content, not encrypted — they're visible to other members by design, the same way a public social feed works anywhere else. Encryption applies specifically to DMs and group chats.

04Direct Messages

DMs are one‑to‑one conversations, protected the way Section 3 describes.

4.1 Starting and managing conversations

Start a new DM from a member's profile, a post, or the member picker in the DM panel.
Conversations are listed with a photo, name, message preview and time, Instagram‑style; unread conversations show a highlighted ring around the avatar.
Swipe a bubble (on mobile) to quickly reply to it.
Delete a message you sent from its context menu; this removes it for both participants.

4.2 Read receipts

Read Receipts show a double‑tick on messages you've sent once the other person has seen them. You can turn this off for yourself in Settings → Customization → Privacy — this is a per‑device, local preference.

4.3 Blocking

Blocking a member immediately stops new DMs between you both in either direction and hides their group‑chat messages from you. Existing conversation history is preserved but new messages won't go through while the block is active. You can block someone from their profile, a message's context menu, or after reporting them.

05Group Chats

Group chats work like DMs but with multiple members, and share the same end‑to‑end encryption described in Section 3.

5.1 Pinning messages

Pin an important message from its context menu so it stays visible in the pinned banner at the top of the chat for everyone in the group. The pin/unpin label always reflects the message's real current state, and the banner updates immediately after you toggle it.

5.2 Moderation inside groups

The same community guidelines and profanity filter apply inside group chats as everywhere else on Social Strand (see Section 9). A muted or suspended member cannot send group messages until their cooldown ends.

06Customization

Settings → Customization lets you personalize how chats look and behave. Changes are saved per device.

Chat Wallpaper & App Background — choose a wallpaper for chat backgrounds, or set a colour/gradient app background, from the built‑in gradient builder.
Message Bubble Color — set the colour used for your own outgoing message bubbles in DMs and group chats.
Bubble Opacity — two sliders control how see‑through your own bubbles and received bubbles are, from 30% to fully opaque, with a live preview before you leave the screen.
Privacy → Read Receipts — toggle the read‑receipt double‑tick on or off. "Reset All" also resets bubble opacity back to 100%/100% alongside every other customization option.

Every post, comment, DM message, group message, and member profile has a Report option in its context menu (⋯ or long‑press). Reports go to the KFS moderation team for review; you'll see a confirmation toast once a report is submitted. See Section 3.3 for exactly what a report on a DM or group message does and doesn't expose.

08Content Ownership, Copyright & Intellectual Property

This Section covers who owns what on Social Strand — your own posts and movies, KFS's own branding and productions, and other people's work — and the rules that protect all three from misuse, whether by a Member or by someone outside KFS entirely.

This Section is a Social Strand‑specific application of the Intellectual Property provisions of KFS's Terms of Use. Nothing here narrows, waives, or replaces those provisions; where this Section is silent, the Terms of Use govern.

8.1 KFS's own content stays KFS's

The KFS name, logo, "Social Strand" branding, this guide's text and design, the Social Strand interface, and any film, trailer, poster, photograph, or promotional material produced or commissioned by KFS itself (KFS Marks) are the exclusive property of KFS. Nothing in this guide, and nothing about being a Member, grants you any right to copy, reproduce, modify, redistribute, or create derivative works from KFS Marks outside Social Strand, except where KFS gives you explicit written permission.

8.2 What you own when you post

You keep full ownership of everything original you create and post — your photographs, videos, movies, scripts, artwork, and captions (Submitted Work). Posting to Social Strand does not transfer copyright to KFS, and it does not make your work available for anyone to reuse.

What you do grant KFS, automatically and for as long as your Submitted Work remains on the platform, is a limited licence — described in full below.

8.3 The licence you grant KFS

By posting or uploading Submitted Work to Social Strand — including feed posts, comments, Collab posts, and Movie Submissions under My Movies / My Works — you grant KFS a non‑exclusive, royalty‑free, revocable licence to:

  • Host, store, and display your Submitted Work within Social Strand and the KFS members' portal, to other Members.
  • Generate thumbnails, previews, and copies technically necessary to operate the feed, DMs, and notifications.
  • Screen or showcase your Submitted Work at official, KSAC‑sanctioned KFS events, internal screenings, or KFS's own promotional channels — with credit to you as the creator, unless you request otherwise.
Note — This licence is limited to the uses above. It does not let KFS sell your work, license it to third parties, use it in commercial advertising outside KFS, or alter its creative content without your consent. The licence ends when you delete the Submitted Work or your account, except for copies already made for an event or promotional use that occurred before deletion, and except where KFS is required to retain a copy as part of an active report or grievance under Sections 7 or 10.

8.4 Movie submissions & collaborative works

Where a Movie Submission is a joint work of multiple credited collaborators (e.g. director, cast, editor), each collaborator is presumed to have consented to its submission and to the licence in Section 8.3 by virtue of being credited; a Member submitting a joint work on behalf of a team represents to KFS that they are authorised to do so. Disputes between collaborators over authorship, credit, or consent are a matter between those Members and are not resolved by KFS moderation, though KFS may suspend public display of a disputed submission pending resolution.

If your movie uses licensed music, stock footage, or any third‑party material, you're responsible for making sure you have the rights to use it — including for a student screening. Sharing something on Social Strand does not, by itself, satisfy any licensing requirement that material may carry.

8.5 Respecting other people's rights

  • Don't post or submit any movie, clip, photo, music, or other material you don't own and don't have permission to use — including pirated films, ripped soundtracks, or another creator's unlicensed footage.
  • Don't repost, re‑upload, screenshot, screen‑record, or otherwise redistribute another Member's Submitted Work outside Social Strand without their consent.
  • Don't remove, obscure, or falsify credit, watermarks, or authorship information on anyone's Submitted Work.
  • Don't claim authorship of, or pass off as your own, a work substantially created by someone else.

8.6 Reporting a copyright or IP concern

If you believe your copyright or another intellectual‑property right has been infringed by something posted on Social Strand — by a Member or by content that shouldn't be there — use the same Report option described in Section 7.1, or contact KFS leadership directly (Section 11) with:

1. What was taken. A description or link to your original work.
2. Where it appears. A link or clear description of the infringing post, comment, or Movie Submission.
3. Your relationship to the work. A statement that you are the rights‑holder, or authorised to act on their behalf.
Note — KFS may remove or restrict access to reported content while a Take‑Down Notice is under review, before a final determination is made, to prevent ongoing harm to the rights‑holder.

8.7 Outside access, scraping & redistribution

Not for anyone outside KFS

Social Strand, this guide, and everything posted on the platform — Members' Submitted Work and KFS Marks alike — are provided exclusively for the private use of current KFS Members and are not licensed for public or outside use. The following are prohibited for outsiders and Members alike:

  • Scraping, bulk‑downloading, mirroring, or automated harvesting of any content from Social Strand
  • Sharing your login, this guide, or platform content with anyone who is not a current KFS Member
  • Republishing, uploading, or distributing Members' movies, photos, or posts on any external site, service, or platform without the creator's and KFS's consent
  • Using KFS Marks or Members' Submitted Work to imply endorsement, affiliation, or sponsorship that does not exist

KFS reserves the right to pursue takedown requests, disciplinary referral to KSAC/KIIT University, and civil or legal remedies against any person — Member or outsider — who infringes these rights, consistent with the Terms of Use.

8.8 Consequences

Copyright or IP infringement by a Member is misconduct under Section 9 and is assessed under the moderation ladder in Section 10.1. Deliberate, repeated, or commercial‑scale infringement — including uploading pirated films or mass‑redistributing Members' work outside the platform — is treated as serious misconduct under Section 10.2 and may result in immediate suspension or permanent account disablement, independent of any separate legal claim the rights‑holder may have.

8.9 No guarantee, and your own responsibility

KFS does not pre‑screen Submitted Work for infringement before it is posted and cannot guarantee that content on Social Strand is free of third‑party rights issues; KFS acts on reports and Take‑Down Notices as described above rather than proactively monitoring content for this purpose. Each Member remains individually responsible for ensuring they hold the necessary rights to anything they post, and agrees to indemnify KFS in accordance with the indemnification provisions of the Terms of Use for any claim arising from their own Submitted Work.

09Community Guidelines & Fair Use Policy

Social Strand exists so KFS members can share work, get feedback, and collaborate. Everything below applies across posts, comments, reactions, DMs, and group chats — including inside end‑to‑end‑encrypted conversations, since misuse there is just as reportable even though KFS can't proactively read the content (Section 3.3).

These guidelines constitute a Social Strand‑specific application of the Community Standards, Member Conduct, and Prohibited Conduct provisions set out in KFS's Terms of Use, and of applicable KSAC and KIIT University rules. Nothing in this Section shall be construed to narrow, waive, or supersede those instruments; conduct that violates the Terms of Use or applicable KSAC/KIIT rules remains a violation notwithstanding its omission below.

9.1 What you can do

  • Share your own projects, work‑in‑progress, and behind‑the‑scenes content.
  • React to and comment constructively on other members' work.
  • Message and group‑chat with other members about collaboration, feedback, or general KFS community topics.
  • Post open collaboration calls describing a role you need filled.
  • Customize your own chat appearance and privacy settings (Section 6).
  • Report content or accounts that break these guidelines, and block anyone you don't want contacting you.
  • Raise a grievance or appeal a decision through the process in Section 10.4.

9.2 What you shouldn't do

  • Post or send harassment, threats, hate speech, or discriminatory content.
  • Use profanity or explicit language in posts, comments, DMs, or group chats — this is filtered automatically and enforced by the moderation ladder in Section 10.
  • Share sexual, graphic, or otherwise explicit/NSFW material.
  • Impersonate another member, staff, or the official KFS account, or attempt to nickname/rename the KFS account.
  • Post spam, scam links, or repeatedly post misleading or off‑topic content to farm engagement.
  • Use group chats or DMs to circumvent a block or continue contact with someone who has blocked you.
  • Attempt to bypass moderation (e.g. spacing out banned words, alternate spellings) to get abusive content past the filter, or attempt to circumvent or interfere with the encryption or security systems described in Section 3.
  • Post or submit movies, images, music, or other material you don't have the rights to use, or redistribute a Member's work outside the platform without consent (Section 8).
  • Submit false reports as a way to harass or silence another member.

10Enforcement, Finality & Grievance Redressal

This Section sets out, in unambiguous terms, the authority KFS exercises over Social Strand accounts, the manner in which that authority is exercised, and the procedure by which a member may seek redress.

10.1 Standard moderation ladder

Most violations — profanity, low‑level rule‑breaking flagged by an automated filter or a routine report — escalate through one shared ladder per account. Each additional violation moves you one step further, regardless of which feature (post, comment, DM, or group message) it happened in:

Violation #Automatic actionWhat it affects
1stWarningMessage is blocked; nothing else changes.
2nd1‑minute mutePosting, comments, DMs and group chat all pause for 1 minute.
3rd2‑minute muteSame as above, longer cooldown.
4th5‑minute muteSame as above, longer cooldown.
5thTemporary account suspensionYou cannot post, DM, or use Social Strand or Group Chats until the timer expires. An appeal link is shown on the lockout screen.
Continued after suspensionPermanent account disableAccount is permanently disabled. Only KFS leadership can review an appeal at that point.
Note — Timers are wall‑clock, not "active use" time — a 5‑minute mute expires 5 minutes after it's issued whether or not you're online.

10.2 Serious misconduct — immediate, harsher action

Zero‑tolerance categories

Certain conduct is serious enough that KFS may act immediately and skip the standard ladder entirely — including an immediate temporary suspension or permanent account disablement on a first occurrence, at KFS's sole discretion. This includes, without limitation:

  • Threats of violence, or content that endangers the safety of any member or third party
  • Hate speech, discriminatory abuse, or targeted harassment
  • Sharing sexual, exploitative, or otherwise illegal content, including content involving minors
  • Impersonating KFS, KFS staff, or the official KFS account
  • Attempting to compromise, bypass, or interfere with Social Strand's encryption, authentication, or moderation systems
  • Doxxing, stalking, or sharing another member's private information without consent
  • Deliberate, repeated, or commercial‑scale copyright or intellectual‑property infringement (Section 8.8)

KFS may preserve relevant account and report information and, where warranted, refer serious misconduct to KSAC, KIIT University disciplinary authorities, law enforcement, or other appropriate authorities, consistent with the Terms of Use.

10.3 Authority & finality of KFS decisions

KFS holds full authority

Social Strand is operated by KFS, and KFS holds full and exclusive authority over its administration, moderation, and enforcement, subject only to the Terms of Use and applicable KSAC/KIIT University rules. This authority includes, without limitation, the right to interpret and apply this guide, issue warnings, mutes, and suspensions, remove content, and permanently disable accounts.

All moderation and administrative decisions relating to Social Strand — including warnings, mutes, temporary suspensions, permanent account disablement, and content removal — are made at KFS's sole discretion. By using Social Strand, you agree that such decisions are final and binding once the applicable appeal process (Section 10.4) has been exhausted, and that continued or repeated misconduct may be weighed cumulatively, including conduct that occurred before this guide's most recent update.

KFS reserves the right to suspend or terminate access to Social Strand, DMs, and group chats for any account, at any time, where KFS reasonably determines a member has violated the Community Guidelines & Fair Use Policy in Section 9, the Content Ownership, Copyright & Intellectual Property provisions in Section 8, engaged in conduct described in Section 10.2, or breached the Terms of Use or applicable KSAC/KIIT rules — independent of, and in addition to, any other membership consequences under KFS's separate governing membership terms, and independent of any referral made under Section 10.2.

10.4 How to raise a grievance or appeal

If you believe a moderation action was applied to you in error, or you want to raise a concern about how a report was handled, you have a direct path to a human review — not just an automated system:

Step 1 — Appeal from the lockout screen. A temporary suspension shows a one‑tap "Request Review" option directly on the suspension screen. This routes straight to the KFS moderation team.
Step 2 — Contact KFS leadership. If your account has been permanently disabled, or you disagree with the outcome of a Step 1 review, contact KFS leadership directly (Section 11) to request a final review.
Step 3 — Decision. KFS leadership's review is the final stage of the grievance process for Social Strand account actions. Its outcome is binding, per Section 10.3.
Note — Raising a grievance in good faith is always welcome and will never itself be treated as a violation. Submitting a report or appeal in bad faith — to harass, silence, or waste the moderation team's time — is itself a violation of Section 9.2.
